# Code of Conduct

arct is used by children and beginners, and built by people of every experience level. Everyone here deserves patience and respect.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- Be kind. Assume good faith. Explain instead of mocking.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, or personal attacks, in any project space.
- Never ask a learner, especially a child, for personal information.
- Critique the work, not the person.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository (it reaches only the maintainers) or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are handled privately.
